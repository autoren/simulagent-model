# V117 Causal Clarification Simulator Results

## Outcome

V117 is a negative causal-feasibility result:

> `causal_simulator_fails_keep_language_and_model_branch_closed`

The frozen simulator replaced V116's abstract duplicate answers with two typed observations generated by
different mechanisms: exact candidate confirmation and catalog-status classification. It swept marginal
correctness, shared failure correlation, and three prior regimes. The planner retained all 17 hypotheses,
could emit only a declared known intent, unsupported, or abstain, and executed nothing. No language, model,
API, training, protected data, or manual record inspection was used.

The two observations were useful in some conditions, but they did not satisfy the preregistered requirement
of improving the frozen 0.7760 regret baseline across every prior at 95% reliability and correlations through
0.50. Perfect observations behaved exactly as expected, so the failure is in robust decision value rather
than implementation or hypothesis retention.

The moderate prior is the only regime that beat or nearly matched the historical regret baseline throughout
the required correlation range. Under the uniform prior, the posterior remained too diffuse to justify any
exact known action, even though unsupported classification was good. Under the strong prior, explicitly
modeling shared failure made the policy abstain on unsupported cases at nonzero correlation. This avoided a
large false-known rate, but it removed the intended unsupported action value and increased regret.

## Misspecification and controls

At actual correlation 0.50, a planner that incorrectly assumed independence had mean regret 1.2056, 0.7968,
and 0.8281 under uniform, moderate, and strong priors. The uniform condition exceeded the frozen 1.125
misspecification ceiling, although false-known probability remained below 10% in all three conditions.

With perfect observations, every prior and correlation condition reached the preregistered mean regret of
0.690625, 96.875% known accuracy, 100% unsupported correctness, and zero false-known probability. This
control confirms that the two typed mechanisms can identify actionable known and unsupported cases in the
limit. At merely 95% reliability, however, their likelihood ratios are not strong enough to support robust
known action under an uninformative prior or robust unsupported action under a strong candidate prior.

## Interpretation and next gate

V117 narrows V116's conditional result. Orthogonal question wording or distinct answer labels do not prove
that a clarification protocol is decision-useful. The observation mechanisms must provide enough calibrated
likelihood separation to overcome both a diffuse prior and a plausible but wrong candidate prior while
remaining robust to shared failures.

Freeze V117 without retuning. It does not authorize a fresh language/model run. A permissible successor is
an aggregate-only, model-free identifiability audit over the frozen hypothesis and loss geometry. That audit
may determine the minimum likelihood-ratio structure any future observation channel would need and whether
an adaptive query policy can satisfy the existing robustness goals in principle. It may not select prompts,
inspect language, fit reliability from V117, grant novel-capability authority, open protected data, or
execute actions.
