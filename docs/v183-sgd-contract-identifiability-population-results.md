# V183 SGD contract-identifiability population results

## Verdict

V183 is a positive text-free source-label and population result. It removes the hidden service-version confound from the frozen V134 asset and creates exactly balanced, identifier-disjoint development and protected roles. It does **not** establish that raw SGD language is utterance-level identifiable.

The source definitions collapse to 14 distinct semantic capability contracts after service version labels are excluded:

- 6 declared known contracts;
- 6 valid-undeclared provisional contracts; and
- 2 unsupported contracts.

No semantic contract crossed truth kinds. Thus no exact capability contract was simultaneously treated as known, provisional, or unsupported merely because it appeared under another numbered source service.

## Hidden source-frame screen

All 240 selected source records reconstructed exactly. The hidden compatibility screen used only the source annotation's normalized active-intent name and non-value slot names; it excluded service, domain, source-definition identity, truth kind, utterances, dialogue text, slot values, and spans.

Every source record retained its target contract, and every compatible set was a singleton:

| Role | Source records | Known identifiable | Provisional identifiable | Unsupported identifiable | Confounded | Missing controls |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Development | 120 | 72 | 36 | 12 | 0 | 12 |
| Protected | 120 | 72 | 36 | 12 | 0 | 12 |

All 24 missing controls retained the complete contract universe and were labeled `INSUFFICIENT`. There were no invalid source records and no cross-kind contract collisions.

This means the source ontology supplies a coherent unique answer once its gold active-intent annotation is known. It does not mean that a human, deterministic parser, or language model can recover that annotation from the utterance. The gold semantic frame is a hidden benchmark-screening oracle and must never be passed to a future proposer.

## Role isolation

Every one of the 66 frozen truth-by-presented-candidate cells contributed exactly two fixtures to development and two to protected use. Each role contains 132 fixtures: 120 source records and 12 missing controls. Opaque role identifiers and source identifiers have zero overlap across roles.

No utterance, dialogue text, slot value, or character span was persisted or manually inspected. V183 used no deterministic policy scoring, model, API, training, ontology registration, trusted-state mutation, service or sensor call, external side effect, action, or execution.

## Decision and next boundary

Freeze:

`freeze_V183_and_authorize_separate_role_isolated_language_extraction_preregistration_only`

The next stage may preregister one exact extraction of development and protected language into separate immutable artifacts. Protected language must remain unread during development. Extraction must include enough source context and schema material to define the future observable interface, while keeping gold service, active intent, truth kind, compatibility set, and evaluation label hidden.

After extraction, a separate text-free protocol must define deterministic controls and an automatic utterance-level compatibility/evidence-sufficiency evaluator before any language is scored. V183 alone does not authorize deterministic policy evaluation, a local model, an API, threshold fitting, calibration, capability registration, trusted-state mutation, action, or execution.
