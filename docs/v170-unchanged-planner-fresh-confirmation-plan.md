# V170 unchanged planner fresh-confirmation plan

## Purpose

V170 applies the complete V167 policy contract without change to all 58 V169 states that contain alias,
composition, and provisional candidates. V169 froze membership before any policy score existed and excluded all
six constraint signatures used by V165's ambiguous development population.

The class-balanced prior, asymmetric loss matrix, query cost 0.1, two-query horizon, optional stopping, seven
policies, and tie-breaks are read from the hashed V167 lock. V167r1's corrected action-only definition of history
dependence is used. V170 neither has nor needs a generative target candidate: its estimand is exact prior expected
cost on the complete version space.

## Outcome rule

Integrity requires all 58 frozen eligible states, exact 64-candidate/three-class inputs, exact rational risk
reconstruction, and pointwise Bayes dominance over every non-oracle control. Opportunity is reported rather than
assumed. A strong confirmation requires at least 44 positive-value states, 15 history-dependent states, 15 strict
improvements over optimal open-loop, and two root queries. Threshold failure produces a retained mixed or negative
boundary result; it does not invite tuning.

No evaluation or human record, model, API, training, registration, trusted-state mutation, service, side effect,
action, or execution is allowed.
