## Bottom line

access gate. It produced the fresh typed program population needed for deterministic candidate/version-space controls.

The frozen branch is `V214_DETERMINISTIC_CONTROL_DESIGN_ELIGIBLE`. This is a structural population result, not a
natural-language or language-model result.

## Research decision

retrieval, typed parsing, bounded synthesis, behavioral-equivalence collapse, contradiction handling, and exact
version-space controls on the development partition before any model becomes eligible.

introduced only if V214 leaves a meaningful, decision-relevant residual after equivalence collapse, and then only as a
fixed-budget candidate generator under a separate lock.
