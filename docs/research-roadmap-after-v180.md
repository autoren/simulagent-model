# Research roadmap after V180

## Established boundary

V178 showed that a single observation of each valuation cannot support unanimous trusted routing when one subsequent
binary observation may be corrupted. V179 then proved, exhaustively, that an indivisible length-three repetition block
with strict majority decoding corrects one global raw error and exactly restores the clean version space. V180 charged
the full block cost (`3 * 0.1 = 0.3`) and found a strong positive development result:

- exact robust mean routed risk `1.6535286935`, versus `2.0` for immediate deferral;
- trusted completion `2/3`;
- all 135 development states strictly improved over immediate deferral;
- pointwise no-worse performance than every operational control;
- exact corruption-route invariance, zero false trusted routing, zero provisional sandbox entry, and zero planner
  authority; and
- the same effective query tree as the V175 clean policy when that policy is repriced at the true triple cost.

The supported claim remains development-only: a fixed error-correcting observation wrapper can preserve safe,
cost-effective certification in the same finite ontology. This is not yet fresh confirmation and does not concern
language, learned observations, real sensors, services, or execution.

## Next active sequence

### V181 — five-constraint confirmation population

Construct the complete five-constraint frame before any planner score is computed:

- enumerate all `C(8,5) * 2^5 = 1,792` states and retain every state;
- each exact version space contains eight candidates;
- define eligibility only by presence of all three frozen classes, preserving the V167 class-balanced prior;
- the prelock structural census identifies 66 eligible states and 528 targets: 66 alias, 72 composition, and 390
  provisional primitive;
- include every candidate in every eligible state with one-third state mass per class and uniform mass within class;
- require zero exact target-context overlap with both V172 and V176;
- disclose candidate-identity reuse explicitly: this is new evidence-context confirmation within one fixed ontology,
  not unseen-concept generalization; and
- compute zero policy, routed-risk, sandbox, model, service, or execution score during population construction.

### V182 — unchanged robust fresh confirmation

Only after V181 freezes successfully, preregister one confirmation using the unchanged V180 mechanism:

- four measurement blocks maximum;
- three raw inspections per block, strict majority decoding, and block cost `0.3`;
- at most one global corrupted raw observation;
- trusted loss zero and defer loss two;
- unanimous alias/composition gate; mixed and unanimous provisional states defer;
- the same immediate, repriced-clean, greedy, fixed open-loop, random-order, exact robust adaptive, and
  non-operational target-informed controls;
- all 66 eligible states and all 528 targets, without selection or subsampling;
- exact dynamic-programming reconstruction, adversarial corruption-route invariance, and V171 sandbox verification;
  and
- safety, primary confirmation, and strong-control gates reported separately.

Any safe negative or mixed result is retained without tuning. A positive result closes the fixed-ontology,
one-corruption repetition-code branch as freshly confirmed. It does not authorize a model or real observation source.

## Research tracks

### Track A — open-world language and learned proposal channels: dormant

The V164 local model result remains negative on novelty recognition, and the later controlled-language sequence shows
that deterministic metadata/grammar can outperform model ranking when the interface is explicit. Neither V180 nor a
future V182 can repair semantic boundary recognition. Reintroduce an LLM only as an untrusted observation or candidate
proposal source under a new utterance-level identifiability and calibration protocol; direct model acceptance remains
forbidden. An API is not required.

### Track B — reversible fixed-ontology sandbox: support mechanism confirmed

Retain V171's preview, invariant, transaction, provenance, restart, rollback, and independent-verification contract.
Repeat it only if routing or persistence semantics change.

### Track C — clean exact certification-aware planning: strongly confirmed and closed

V175/V177 are the clean reference. Do not rerun, retune, or select states from them.

### Track E — bounded observation-corruption robustness: active until V182

V178 is the single-pass impossibility boundary, V179 is the coding-theoretic feasibility result, and V180 is the
cost-aware development result. V181/V182 are the only active successor sequence.

## Standing authority boundary

- Only deterministic unanimous evidence may enter the trusted sandbox.
- Planner, model, posterior, oracle, hidden target, and provisional primitive never authorize commit.
- Population construction, planner development, and confirmation remain separately locked.
- No permanent ontology registration, real sensor or service call, trusted real-state mutation, external side effect,
  or execution is authorized.
