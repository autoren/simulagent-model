# V71 sensor-codebook source feasibility and development preregistration

V70 established that retaining a posterior over a project-authored action/dynamics remapping can matter across several external POMDPs. V71 tests a materially different boundary: uncertainty about the semantics of delivered observation labels while source dynamics remain fixed. This is a prospective development-to-confirmation sequence, not another analysis of V69 or V70 outcomes.

The external source is the official `cassandra/pomdp-solve` repository pinned at commit `4f5d399f260f7789bc1d95baab069eef5c93aaf9`. Its 19 example `.POMDP` files are frozen byte-for-byte. The source is CC BY-NC 4.0, so this stage is research-only and does not claim commercial-use permission.

The partition was made from provenance and strict source validity only. `concert.POMDP`, `ejs1.POMDP`, and `manuel-hartman.2013-09-19.POMDP` form the fresh development set. `ejs2.POMDP` and `ejs3.POMDP` are protected related confirmation models; `bridge-repair.POMDP`, `parr95.95.POMDP`, and `uav-search.raissa-bravo.orig.POMDP` are protected novel confirmation models. Five prior-domain or byte-identical sources are excluded to prevent reuse. Six malformed or non-normalized sources are excluded without repair, renormalization, or replacement.

The latent variable is static and binary: `canonical_dominant` or `reversed_dominant`, with prior `(0.5, 0.5)`. Under the pinned source observation order, `reverse(j)=K-1-j` and reliability is fixed prospectively at `rho=0.85`. The canonical point model emits the source label with weight `rho` and its reversed partner with weight `1-rho`; the reversed point model swaps those weights. Transitions are identical to the source in both point models. Observation-conditioned source rewards are first integrated exactly under the source observation distribution, before the delivered-label channel is applied.

This construction has an important hard property: the two point models have identical observation support at every action and successor state. Because they also share transitions and initial state support, a history reachable under the exact joint model must remain on-support under either point filter. V71 therefore forbids fallback rather than totalizing it; any support violation rejects the design or implementation.

If source feasibility passes, a resource-only preflight may be written and audited. It may use model dimensions and support masks, but it may not construct policies, values, regrets, information-gain scores, or protected histories. Development evaluation is not authorized by the source lock itself.

The frozen development design uses the complete positive-probability depth-0 and depth-1 public-prefix census, horizon three, and exact categorical belief over `(sensor codebook, source state)`. Controls are posterior MAP, posterior sampling, open loop, and myopic one-step planning, all compared with exact Bayes-adaptive planning. There is no SMC2, neural model access, adapter training, or human record access.

Development passes only if all three models show an exact-BA/MAP root-action disagreement, at least two show material MAP regret, at least one shows material posterior-sampling regret, and maximum normalized MAP regret reaches `0.01`. Materiality is frozen at `0.005`. Completeness, validity, normalization, finiteness, shared support, and zero-selection gates are noncompensatory. A failed gate ends V71 before protected confirmation. Passing development authorizes only a separately preregistered protected-confirmation design; it never authorizes reading protected outcomes directly.
