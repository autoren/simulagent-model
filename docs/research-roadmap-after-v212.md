# Research roadmap after V212

## Evidence now frozen

V212 closed the model-free representational-diagnosis oracle with branch `V213_DESIGN_ELIGIBLE`. Over a complete
256-behavior domain it exactly recovered candidate equivalence classes, evidence states, representation-relative
expressibility, boundary witnesses, and shadow decisions. Forced invention, forced merging, a closed-world base
language, and universal abstention all had material regret.

The result changes the immediate question from “can these distinctions be defined?” to “can a fresh, role-separated
population expose them without leaking executable truth into public observations?”

## Primary sequence: G2/B2a

### V213 — fresh programmatic concept population

V213 should prospectively freeze and then generate a fresh population whose roles are separated before materialization:

- a generator role creates hidden executable concept programs from the frozen semantic algebra;
- a public-observation role emits definitions, examples, counterexamples, references, and boundary evidence without
  access to the final diagnostic label;
- a hidden-truth role records exact behavioral class, expressibility in `L0` and `L+`, evidence state, boundary
  witnesses, and the correct shadow action;
- a split role assigns development and protected groups before any downstream extractor is fit or evaluated.

The population should include exact aliases, equivalent rewrites, compositions, near aliases, broader/narrower
relations, missing operators, irreducibles relative to the frozen languages, arbitrary reference-grounded symbols,
ambiguity, contradiction, and outside descriptions. Renamings and counterfactual presentations should be grouped so
equivalent records cannot cross splits.

Passing V213 means the population is structurally valid, role-isolated, nonleaking, balanced, identifiable where
intended, and genuinely ambiguous or contradictory where intended. It does not itself authorize a model.

### V214 — deterministic candidate and version-space controls

On the frozen V213 development split, exhaust deterministic methods first:

- exact and approximate retrieval;
- typed parsing;
- bounded program synthesis;
- behavioral-equivalence collapse;
- contradiction detection;
- boundary-query selection;
- calibrated deferral for outside or underdetermined cases.

The principal output is the exact residual version space, not merely top-1 accuracy. V214 must distinguish failure to
retrieve the correct equivalence class from failure to rank, calibrate, or defer.

### Conditional V215 — bounded local LLM candidate generation

An LLM becomes eligible only if V214 leaves a meaningful residual that is both nontrivial and decision-relevant. Its
role is candidate generation under a fixed budget. The primary measure is incremental oracle-class recall after
equivalence collapse, together with false-class proposal rate, candidate-set size, completeness/truncation, and
downstream regret. It receives no authority to register a concept, mutate trusted state, call a service, or execute an
action.

Thinking effort and token budget must be separately controlled. Incomplete reasoning is an observed generation
failure, not positive abstention, unless an independently specified controller maps it to a safe action and reports the
mapping separately.

## Parallel feasibility track: external semantic resources

A metadata-first census may run in parallel after a separate prospective lock. It can assess versioned ontologies,
alignment benchmarks, competency questions, logical definitions, examples, and change histories for suitability as
retrospective reconstruction evidence.

Any later payload-bearing study must keep source roles explicit:

- existing curated resources may provide historical definitions and version changes;
- alignments and mappings may provide provisional correspondences;
- executable logical content may supply behavioral tests;
- none of these establishes the intended meaning of a genuinely new speaker without appropriate speaker or expert
  evidence.

This track should not delay V213/V214, and it must not silently convert historical curation into a prospective human
intent claim.

## Downstream integration track

If provisional candidates survive V214 or conditional V215, reuse the already frozen reversible sandbox and
certificate-aware planner. Candidate meanings remain shadow-only until the existing evidence/certificate rules allow a
decision. Do not rebuild execution machinery or grant the language component direct tool authority.

## Deferred claim: B2c

Novel ontology acquisition in the strong sense remains deferred. Model-only or resource-only experiments can study
hypothesis generation, behavioral equivalence, evidence gathering, and safe provisional retention. They cannot claim
that a newly induced meaning is the correct human meaning without a valid source of semantic adjudication.

## Current priority

1. Design and prospectively freeze V213.
2. Materialize and outcome-freeze V213 if its design audit passes.
3. Design V214 from the frozen population.
4. In parallel only when separately locked, run a metadata-first external-resource feasibility census.
5. Introduce a bounded local LLM only if V214 proves a meaningful residual.
