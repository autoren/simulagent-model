# V70 confirmatory multi-environment preregistration

V69 passed every prospectively frozen development gate on four previously exposed models. V70 therefore freezes a confirmatory replication before computing any policy value on the nine valid untouched source models assigned in V68: three structurally related models and six novel models. The invalid `paint.POMDP` source remains excluded for its pre-outcome parser-semantic defect.

The V69 dominant forward/backward action-remapping family, priors, θ support, exact horizon-three planner, 65/129-node convergence check, controls, point-policy totalization, normalization, and 0.005 materiality threshold are unchanged. Canonical cycles are frozen from semantic directional order where available and source action order otherwise.

Every positive-probability depth-0 and depth-1 history will be retained. Before census construction or scoring, a resource-only audit must estimate the complete record count and exact-operation bounds from state/action/observation dimensions. It may defer the entire run but may not drop a model or inspect any policy outcome.

The primary decision is model-level. At least three of nine models must show an exact-BA/MAP root-action disagreement and material MAP regret, including at least one structurally related and two novel models. At least two models must show material posterior-sampling regret, and maximum normalized MAP regret must reach 0.01. Numerical, completeness, source, and zero-selection gates are noncompensatory.

The project-authored family across nine environments is Tier A. The terminating/nonterminating cheese pair is also reported as a separate Tier B stress comparison, but it is not pooled into or described as an externally supplied uncertainty-family replication. No confirmatory outcome is authorized until design, resource, census, and evaluator locks all exist.
