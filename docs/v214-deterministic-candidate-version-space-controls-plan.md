# V214 deterministic candidate and version-space controls plan

## Purpose

V213 produced a fresh, role-separated typed program population. V214 now establishes how much of its development
partition is closed by deterministic mechanisms before any language model can become eligible.

The expected outcome is not assumed. A zero residual closes the model branch for this representation; a sufficiently
large, expressible, decision-relevant residual can authorize a separate bounded local candidate-generator design.

## Input firewall and development subsplit

V214 reconstructs only the 80 V213 development groups from the frozen V213 generator algorithm and seeds. It does not
open the frozen V213 public-record, sealed-truth, or split artifacts and does not construct protected group records.

Before control execution, a frozen hash ranking splits the development groups within every family into:

- four fit groups and 16 fit records;
- four evaluation groups and 16 evaluation records.

Across ten families this yields 40 fit groups/160 fit records and 40 evaluation groups/160 evaluation records. All four
variants of a group remain together. The control worker receives fit public records and fit truth, but only evaluation
public records. Predictions are frozen before evaluation truth is joined.

## Frozen control ladder

1. `EXACT_STRUCTURAL_CEILING`: the frozen V212 complete-domain resolver, explicitly labeled as an oracle-aligned
   ceiling.
2. `NORMALIZED_EXACT_RETRIEVAL_K8`: group-disjoint exact retrieval after identity, commutativity, ordering, and arbitrary
   symbol normalization; at most eight proposed behavioral classes.
3. `TYPED_APPROX_RETRIEVAL_K8`: typed feature/Jaccard retrieval from four nearest fit groups, with at most eight proposed
   behavioral classes.
4. `BOUNDED_L0_LPLUS_SYNTHESIS`: enumeration of the 15 behaviors expressible by the frozen `L0`/`L+` catalog, plus
   deterministic contradiction and reference-constraint handling.
5. `FULL_CONSTRAINT_PROPAGATION`: complete filtering over all 256 behavioral classes.
6. `DETERMINISTIC_STACK`: typed parsing and bounded synthesis with complete constraint propagation as the frozen
   fallback.

Outside-interface detection is not treated as a semantic candidate. No-proposal, contradiction, and outside-interface
states remain distinct. Incomplete retrieval is not mislabeled as contradiction or positive abstention.

## Metrics

For every method V214 records:

- oracle-class recall at the frozen candidate budget;
- exact version-space accuracy;
- false-class proposal rate and count;
- average and maximum candidate-set size;
- evidence-status, expressibility-set, and shadow-action accuracy;
- normalized shadow-decision regret;
- group-variant invariance;
- prediction count and budget compliance;
- residual records and groups by family, evidence state, expressibility state, and failure mechanism.

The structural ceiling and full propagation must reconstruct all evaluation cases exactly. At least one bounded control
must remain imperfect so the ladder is empirically separated rather than six names for one computation.

## Model-eligibility rule

The unchanged deterministic stack is the branch selector. A record is model-eligible only if:

- it is neither outside, intentionally ambiguous, nor contradictory;
- the correct oracle class is absent or the shadow action is wrong;
- the correct class is expressible from the public typed evidence;
- the error contributes positive normalized decision regret.

A local model design requires at least eight residual groups and average normalized regret of at least `0.02`. Anything
smaller freezes deterministic closure with zero model eligibility. Thresholds, budgets, normalization, tie-breaks, and
rules cannot be changed after the design lock.

## Claim and authority boundary

V214 is development-only and programmatic. It makes no claim about natural-language understanding. It authorizes no
V213 protected access, external ontology payload, local/API model, training, registration, trusted-state mutation,
service/tool call, authority, side effect, action, or execution.
