# V181 five-constraint robust-confirmation population plan

## Objective

Freeze a new exact evidence-context population before evaluating V180's robust mechanism. Enumerate every five-of-eight
valuation subset and every binary outcome assignment, retaining all `1,792` states. Eligibility uses only whether the
exact version space contains all three frozen expressibility classes.

## Targets and weights

The structural census is fixed before policy scoring: 66 states are eligible and contain 528 targets (66 alias, 72
composition, and 390 provisional primitive). Include every target. Give each class one third of state-level mass and
distribute that mass uniformly within class. No state or target may be selected using a planner, policy, sandbox, risk,
or downstream outcome.

## Freshness and boundary

The exact signature is the canonical five-constraint context plus the target's complete eight-bit truth table. Require
zero overlap with every V172 and V176 target-context signature. Candidate identities are intentionally reused because
the ontology is fixed; this is context-generalization evidence, not unseen-concept evidence.

V181 builds only shadow version spaces and target identities. It computes no policy score or routed risk and runs no
sandbox transaction, model, API, sensor, service, registration, real-state mutation, side effect, or execution. A pass
authorizes only separate preregistration of one unchanged V180 robust confirmation.
