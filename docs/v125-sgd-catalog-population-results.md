# V125 SGD Catalog and Population Results

## Outcome

V125 passed and froze an eleven-choice complete safe composite universe:

- six exact known service-intent choices across Hotels, Movies, and Services;
- three valid-undeclared composite hypotheses, one per visible domain;
- one unsupported composite spanning four unseen test domains;
- one insufficient-evidence control.

All 4,881 train candidates for the six declared intents are locked as the future character-retrieval training
population. A separate 576-record evaluation population was hash-selected before language extraction:
192 known (32 per declared intent), 192 novel-valid (64 per visible domain), and 192 unsupported (48 per
unseen domain).

The catalog and populations are identifier-disjoint where required, cover all six known pairs, three novel
domains, and four unsupported domains, and contain no utterances, dialogues, tokens, or slot values. No
language, model, API, training, authority, or execution was used.

This is population preregistration, not performance evidence. The next authorized action is only a separately
locked cross-dataset retrieval-geometry selectivity design. That design must freeze extraction, the retrieval
mechanism, trigger, clarification model, costs, and outcome gates before any selected language is opened.
