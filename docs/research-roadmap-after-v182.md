# Research roadmap after V182

## Closed fixed-ontology robustness result

The exact symbolic control stack now has a complete clean-to-corrupted sequence:

- V175/V177 strongly confirm certification-aware adaptive planning under clean deterministic observations.
- V171 confirms the reversible sandbox, transaction, provenance, restart, rollback, and independent-verification
  boundary.
- V178 proves that single-pass binary observations cannot support unanimous trusted routing when one later observation
  may be corrupted.
- V179 proves that fixed triple repetition with strict majority exactly corrects one global raw error and restores the
  clean version space.
- V180 shows that the error-correcting wrapper remains cost-effective on development contexts.
- V181/V182 strongly confirm the unchanged robust mechanism on a separately frozen five-constraint population: exact
  robust risk `1.4418181818` versus `2.0` for immediate deferral, trusted completion `2/3`, improvement on all 66
  states, and exact safety.

The supported claim is narrow but now fresh: within one finite ontology and a declared one-corruption binary channel,
fixed repetition-code redundancy preserves cost-effective trusted certification. It is not evidence about language,
learned observations, unseen concepts, real sensors, or deployment.

Do not rerun, tune, or extend V178–V182 in place. Multiple corruptions, non-binary channels, or changed costs are new
questions requiring new populations and locks.

## Next active track: externally grounded untrusted semantic observations

The most informative open question is no longer whether the exact planner and gate work. It is whether externally
authored language can supply useful evidence to that system without becoming authority.

The learned component should not output a final accepted capability. It should emit a bounded, typed candidate set or
semantic observation with explicit `INSUFFICIENT`/`OUTSIDE_CATALOG` possibilities. A deterministic layer retains every
compatible hypothesis, validates schemas, chooses trusted clarification or inspection questions, and alone applies the
unanimous gate. Model errors may increase questions or deferral; they must not directly authorize routing, state
mutation, registration, action, or execution.

### Recommended sequence

1. **Externally grounded population feasibility.** Pin a primary-source task-oriented-dialogue corpus with service and
   intent schemas, utterances, dialogue context, and structured annotations. Define capability identity from the
   service/intent/slot contract rather than intent name alone. Freeze train/development/protected identities before
   reading model outcomes.
2. **Utterance-level identifiability audit.** For each record, enumerate every catalog schema compatible with the
   available structured context. If one status or capability is not identifiable from the declared input, truth must
   be `INSUFFICIENT`; do not force a hidden service-version distinction into a known/novel label. Report and retain
   confounded records rather than deleting them after model scores.
3. **Deterministic controls first.** Compare exact schema lookup, lexical/schema retrieval, complete-catalog candidate
   retention, always-clarify, and oracle controls. Define any model residual from predictions only before language-model
   scoring.
4. **One local-model proposal condition.** Use a pinned local model with direct or low bounded reasoning and reserved
   final-output budget. Output is a candidate set, never an action. Truncation, malformed output, or unsupported fields
   become `INSUFFICIENT`. An API is optional as a later capacity control, not a dependency.
5. **Correlated-error stress.** Do not treat repeated calls, paraphrases, or small/large models as independent evidence
   without a causal justification. Evaluate common-mode semantic errors and require deterministic clarification before
   trusted routing.
6. **Decision integration only after semantic gates pass.** Freeze the observation likelihood or conservative
   set-membership rule, costs, question menu, routed loss, and controls before measuring planner value. Keep V171's
   authority boundary unchanged.

### Noncompensatory semantic gates

- complete target-candidate recall on trusted routes;
- zero or prospectively bounded false-known acceptance;
- separate known, outside-catalog, unsupported, and insufficient-evidence behavior;
- calibration/selective risk adequate for the frozen routed-loss geometry;
- exact schema validity and no hallucinated intent or slot authority;
- candidate-set size and clarification cost reported alongside accuracy;
- correlated-error stress does not create false trusted routes; and
- zero registration, real service, trusted real-state mutation, side effect, or execution.

## Research tracks

### Track A — open-world language: reopen only under the new observation architecture

V164's direct local residual classifier remains negative and closed. Reopening Track A is justified only because the
new architecture changes the estimand: set-valued evidence plus deterministic clarification, not direct status
classification or model confidence as authority. Old protected MASSIVE data remains sealed unless a new protocol
prospectively authorizes it; a fresh schema-grounded source is preferred.

### Track B — reversible sandbox: confirmed support mechanism

Retain V171 unchanged. Revalidate only if input, routing, transaction, or persistence semantics change.

### Track C — exact representation and clean planning: confirmed reference

V165–V177 remain the model-free reference. They define the complete hypothesis, evidence, planning, and gate semantics
against which a language channel must be compared.

### Track E — one-corruption symbolic robustness: confirmed and closed

V178–V182 are complete. Preserve them as the coding-theoretic observation control.

### Track F — learned/untrusted semantic observation integration: next active track

Begin with source and identifiability feasibility, then deterministic controls, then at most one local proposal
condition. Do not start with an API, ensemble, adapter, training run, or direct execution study.

## Standing authority boundary

- Model output is evidence or a proposal, never truth or authority.
- Only deterministic validated unanimity may route a trusted alias or composition.
- `provisional_primitive` and outside-catalog candidates remain shadow-only.
- Planner, posterior, oracle, hidden target, confidence, and model never authorize commit.
- Permanent registration, real services, trusted real-state mutation, side effects, and execution remain forbidden.
