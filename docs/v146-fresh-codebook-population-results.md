# V146 fresh controlled codebook-scoring population results

V146 passed every prospective population, codebook, leakage, and access gate.

- The catalog has four known, three valid-undeclared, one unsupported, and one insufficient-evidence choice.
- Six contrastive families produce 48 six-stage groups and 288 fixtures.
- Development and test each contain 24 complete groups and 144 fixtures.
- Language classes contain 48 known-familiar, 48 known-unfamiliar, 48 ambiguous, 48 known-clarified, 32 novel-valid, 32 novel-valid-clarified, 16 unsupported, and 16 unsupported-clarified fixtures.
- Every decisive row has its truth as the exact singleton compatible set; every ambiguous row has its exact registered pair.
- Public and hidden fixture IDs align exactly, and public rows contain no hidden truth, stage, family, language-class, or compatible-set fields.
- The 14-code registered certificate codebook covers every oracle certificate and final choice.
- Exact conversation overlap with the combined V135 and V142 populations is zero.
- Model loads, generations, API calls, training runs, and executions are zero.

The population uses new catalog IDs, domains, templates, slot values, and conversations. It does not reuse V142 development and does not open V142 test for model evaluation.

Passing authorizes only preregistration of a closed-alternative scoring protocol. It does not authorize any model scoring or generation until that protocol is separately audited and frozen.
