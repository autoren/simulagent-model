# Research roadmap after V221r1

## Frozen conclusion

V221/V221r1 closes the V220 retrospective Mondo residual branch. Exact family expansion at the frozen accepted budget
retained the complete oracle version space across all 325 evaluation groups. The model-eligible residual is zero, so
the conditional local-model experiment is cancelled rather than run.

This is a positive result for deterministic reconstruction over externally published versioned artifacts. It does
not answer the B2c question of what a new speaker means by an unfamiliar expression.

## Track status

### Track A — retrospective ontology reconstruction: closed positive

V219A selected an untouched Mondo release pair, V220 built the fresh role-separated transition population, and
V221/V221r1 proved deterministic candidate and version-space sufficiency. No protected confirmation or LLM condition
is justified for this task because there is no decision-relevant deterministic residual.

### Track B2a/B2b — representational mechanics: established development evidence

The V212–V214 style sequence remains useful as a general protocol: prove equivalence and identifiability, generate
role-separated executable concepts, and exhaust deterministic candidate/version-space controls before considering a
model. V219A–V221 provide a resource-backed instance of that logic for historical ontology change.

Further project-authored populations may test new representation operators or decision mechanisms, but they must be
described as mechanism studies. They cannot validate real speaker meaning.

### Track B2c — external semantics and new-speaker intent: open but evidence-limited

Existing ontologies can supply concept definitions, synonyms, mappings, provenance, releases, and lifecycle relations.
These are valuable candidate and historical-state evidence. They do not by themselves determine whether a novel
utterance is an unusual expression of an existing concept, a genuinely new concept, unsupported, or underspecified.

The next justified goal is therefore a **metadata- and provenance-first external resource eligibility census**, not a
model run. It should search for a source that independently supplies all of the following:

1. natural utterances or queries paired with versioned concept/catalog context;
2. explicit speaker, domain-expert, adjudicated, or operationally grounded semantic judgments;
3. known-versus-novel-versus-insufficient boundary cases, not merely nearest-label classification;
4. group identities that permit leakage-resistant calibration, evaluation, and protected roles;
5. machine-readable provenance and immutable versioning;
6. compatible reuse terms; and
7. enough boundary diversity to support decision-relevant evaluation.

The census must inspect metadata, schemas, documentation, licenses, and provenance before opening task language. It
must not infer gold judgments from catalog absence, fabricate reviewers, or treat an LLM's confidence/agreement as
independent semantic truth.

V223 now instantiates this census around archived semantic-change adjudication rather than repeating V215's release
artifact study or V208's behavioral-abstention dataset search. Its four frozen workflow families are Mondo term
requests, Gene Ontology term requests, Schema.org proposals, and Wikidata property proposals. The strongest
pre-audit candidate is Mondo because its official workflow explicitly connects user requests, community comment,
curation-team review, pull requests, new identifiers, and monthly releases while exposing duplicate, clarification,
and out-of-scope states. This remains a hypothesis until the V223 design audit and formal metadata census pass.

Even a positive result would narrow B2c to structured ontology-change requests. It would not establish spontaneous
conversational intent recognition. A source-specific successor must inspect disposition metadata and prove usable
strata before opening request language.

## Branch rule after the census

- If a qualifying external source exists, freeze a source-specific acquisition and identifiability protocol before
  opening its utterances. Deterministic controls run first; a local LLM may later become a non-authoritative candidate
  generator only for a prospectively defined residual.
- If a partially qualifying source exists, use it only for the claims its annotations actually support and document
  the missing semantic axis.
- If no source qualifies, freeze B2c as externally unvalidated. Continue only model-free mechanism studies or
  retrospective resource reconstruction, without simulating human validation or relabeling synthetic judgments as
  real-world semantic evidence.

## Preserved boundaries

- V220 protected records remain sealed; a zero residual does not justify opening them.
- No V222 local-model branch is authorized for the Mondo retrospective task.
- An API is not needed for the next stage.
- Existing local-model results remain proposal/interface evidence only.
- No model may define gold semantics, calibrate its own likelihood from confidence, register a concept, mutate the
  trusted ontology, authorize service action, or execute.
- Human-like simulated responses may test protocol mechanics but cannot satisfy independent-speaker or expert-review
  claims.
