# V191 fresh language-to-menu population results

## Verdict

V191 passed every availability, contract-coverage, dialogue-freshness, source-mapping, missing-control, and access gate.
It freezes a new text-free development population for testing whether language can non-authoritatively reduce the
V190 menu. It does not yet contain or evaluate language.

## Population

The population contains 84 observed SGD records and 14 missing controls, for 98 fixtures total. Every one of the 14
frozen capability contracts contributes exactly six observed records:

- 36 known records;
- 36 provisional records;
- 12 unsupported records; and
- 14 insufficient-evidence controls with no source record or target.

All 84 source identifiers map exactly to their frozen full-contract targets. All selected dialogues are unique.

## Freshness

V191 excluded every dialogue that appeared in either V183 role, rather than excluding only the exact selected turns.
Consequently:

- overlap with V183 source candidate identifiers is zero;
- overlap with any V183 development or protected dialogue is zero; and
- within-V191 dialogue overlap is zero.

The limiting contract, `AddAlarm`, had exactly six eligible untouched dialogues, and all six were selected under the
prospectively fixed salted ordering. No language, semantic frame, slot value, span, prediction, or outcome influenced
selection.

## Access and authority boundary

Public identities contain only opaque record ID, role, and observation availability. Source candidate identifiers and
full-contract targets are isolated in a hidden automatic-scoring artifact. No utterance or dialogue text, slot value,
frame, or span was persisted. Manual language inspection, protected-language reading, policy scoring, local/API model
use, training, ontology registration, trusted mutation, service calls, side effects, action, and execution were zero.
