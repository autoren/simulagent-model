# V136 Controlled Clarification-value Audit Plan

## Question

Does the family-specific clarification structure frozen by V135 have positive decision value under the
historical asymmetric open-world costs, and how reliable must its binary answer be?

## Model-free audit

For each of five V135 families and three priors on its presented known candidate, compare the exact Bayes
terminal decision without asking against one family-specific clarification question costing 0.30. The
answer names the left or right live hypothesis and follows a symmetric binary error channel. Evaluate a
0.0005 reliability grid from 0.5 through 1.0 and freeze the first reliability at which asking strictly
improves expected cost.

The terminal choices are shadow typed decisions only. A valid-undeclared decision does not register or run
a capability. The full safe hypothesis representation is retained and execution remains zero.

Clear cases use a degenerate truth belief and must prefer skipping the query. Ambiguous cases must prefer
asking both with a perfect answer and at 95% answer reliability. At 95%, every condition must improve by at
least 0.299 cost, false-known probability on the non-known right truth must remain at most 5.1%, and a safe
non-known terminal decision must occur at least 94.9% of the time. The worst query-benefit threshold must be
at most 95%.

Passing authorizes only a prospective direct-versus-thinking model protocol. It does not authorize a model
run, V134 language access, APIs, induction, training, authority, action, or execution.
