# V40 independent declared-language compiler confirmation

## Frozen claim

V40 tests whether the already frozen V39 compiler transfers exactly to fresh, explicitly declared predicate lexicons, operator cues, entity namespaces, and record combinations under the same controlled grammar.

This is a confirmation of a language-to-symbol interface contract. It is not evidence for open-ended natural-language understanding, ontology discovery, learned grounding, sequential dynamics, or general world-model learning.

## Independence

The V39 compiler and outcome are frozen before V40 construction. V40 will use an independent generator and independent reference-target construction. It may call the V39 compiler only after the V40 population has passed structural audit and been hash-sealed. The compiler may not be changed, wrapped with case-specific repairs, or selected using confirmation results.

The suite contains 12 fresh ontology packs. Each pack has fresh entity names, entity-type names, unary and relational predicate identifiers, direct and inverse lexical forms, and a fresh declared cue for each of the five fixed semantic operations. The exact field labels, three separator forms, semantic operation inventory, and fixed truth table remain within the declared V39 scope.

## Population

Each ontology pack contributes exactly 120 core records, crossing five operations, two focus positions, three decoy kinds, two relation orientations, and two lexical signs. Cue alias, separator, entity count, relation choice, and argument reversal are deterministically balanced across those cells. Ontology pack is the reporting unit.

The safety suite contributes two cases per condition per pack for mixed separators, two focus fields, an unknown predicate lexeme, an unknown operator cue, and duplicate operation fields: 120 safety records total.

## Gates

Coverage, exact parse, compiled truth, every ontology-pack exact-parse rate, every operation/sign exact-parse rate, overall safety, and every safety-condition rate must all equal 1.000. There is one sealed confirmation evaluation and no selection or repair from its outcome.

## Interpretation

- All gates pass: accept exact declared-language compilation as a stable interface component and proceed to a fresh end-to-end relational-mechanic confirmation using that interface.
- Any semantic gate fails: reject transfer beyond the V39 construction; do not repair and repeat V40.
- Any safety gate fails: reject the fail-closed claim; do not repair and repeat V40.

Open paraphrase, undeclared concepts, and ontology induction remain explicitly outside the claim.
