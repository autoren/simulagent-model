# V58 preregistration: human-authored language over known concepts

V58 isolates the report's second language stage. The ontology, semantic AST, and exact compiler/executor contract remain known and frozen; only the surface wording changes. No generated or model-written sentence may count as human-authored evidence.

## Collection and split

Two pilot writers may supply development text but are excluded from evaluation. At least ten different evaluation writers contribute 60 accepted utterances each. Each prompt specifies a known V40 meaning and asks the writer to express it naturally. Two blinded validators independently check whether the submitted text expresses the prompted AST; disagreements are adjudicated, and genuinely ambiguous items move to a separate abstention stratum.

Evaluation is held out jointly by writer and construction family. Random utterance splits are forbidden. Construction families cover assertion, lexical negation, denial, double denial, contrastive focus, unresolved status, direct and inverse relations, argument reversal, and distractor scope. Every accepted item carries anonymous author and collection provenance plus consent/license attestation.

## Candidate boundary and controls

The candidate interface must be frozen before any evaluation-author text is visible. Only pilot-author text may be used for development. The exact AST is the primary outcome, with compiled truth, relation order, negative composition, selective accuracy, and abstention safety reported separately. The frozen V39 declared compiler, lexical lookup, majority AST, shuffled-label, and shallow surface-feature baselines prevent an apparent gain from being mistaken for structural transfer.

## Decision rule

All 16 gates are noncompensatory. Overall performance cannot compensate for a bad author or construction family. A pass qualifies human-authored surface transfer over known concepts only. V57 and V58 must each pass separately before a joint new-surface/new-concept 2×2 design may be preregistered.
