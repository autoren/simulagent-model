# V213 fresh programmatic concept population plan

## Purpose

V212 proved that behavioral equivalence, representation-relative expressibility, evidence status, boundary witnesses,
and shadow decisions can be diagnosed exactly in the frozen finite domain. V213 now constructs the fresh,
role-separated population required before deterministic candidate/version-space controls can be tested.

V213 is not a natural-language study. Public records are typed JSON evidence programs. “Protected” means reserved from
downstream method development; it does not mean that the population's own one-shot structural verifier can never check
its sealed executable truth.

## Role separation

The prospectively frozen generator has four roles:

1. **Blueprint generator:** deterministically creates public evidence blueprints and a separate sealed executable truth
   artifact from frozen seeds and algorithms.
2. **Public projection worker:** receives only public blueprints and a frozen public field list. It never receives the
   sealed truth, concept-family label, target behavior, expected candidate set, or expected action.
3. **Split role:** assigns opaque concept groups to development or protected partitions before any downstream fitting,
   with exact within-family balance.
4. **Structural verifier:** only after the public projection is hashed and frozen, joins the sealed truth to verify the
   population. It fits no method and reports no protected downstream benchmark.

All transformations of one semantic concept remain in the same opaque group and therefore in the same split.

## Population

The frozen design creates 12 concept groups in each of ten families:

- exact aliases;
- existing `L0` compositions;
- one-boundary near aliases;
- broader/narrower relations;
- missing `L+` operators;
- behaviors irreducible relative to `L0` and `L+`;
- arbitrary symbols grounded by in-episode references;
- genuinely ambiguous evidence;
- contradictory evidence;
- outside descriptions.

Every group receives four deterministic presentation regimes: base, renamed-or-nested, reversed-order, and equivalent
rewrite. This gives 120 groups and 480 records. Within every family, eight groups (32 records) are assigned to
development and four groups (16 records) to protected, for 320 development and 160 protected records overall.

Small finite classes may recur across groups; V213 promises group-disjoint, not behavior-disjoint, splitting. The exact
target behavior is not the unit of independence for aliases because only three registered primitives exist.

## Freshness and truth

Group and case identifiers are opaque salted hashes and cannot contain family or target names. V213 uses new seeds and
does not copy V212 identifiers or records. Programmatic selection is deterministic:

- finite candidate pools are hash-ranked under frozen family seeds;
- near aliases are generated by one-bit changes from registered anchors;
- irreducibles are selected only from behaviors outside both frozen languages;
- ambiguous cases omit exactly one world and retain exactly two behaviors;
- contradiction patterns alternate among conflicting observations and conflicting reference relations;
- reference-grounded symbols alternate same/complement evidence;
- outside descriptions impose no behavioral constraint and expose no authorized boundary question.

The hidden truth is derived from these generator instructions, while the public-only V212 oracle independently resolves
the projected evidence. Agreement is therefore checked rather than assumed.

## Gates

V213 passes only if all of the following hold exactly:

- 120 groups, 480 records, ten families, 12 groups and 48 records per family;
- four variants per group with one split per group;
- 80 development and 40 protected groups, yielding 320/160 records;
- eight development and four protected groups in every family;
- zero group overlap across splits and zero V212 identifier reuse;
- zero hidden-field leakage through the public blueprint or public records;
- exact candidate-set, evidence-status, expressibility-set, and shadow-action reconstruction;
- exact within-group semantic consistency and transformation invariance;
- complete candidate-pair and comparison-anchor boundary witnesses;
- no invalid, duplicate, non-opaque, or unaccounted identifiers;
- a public projection freeze before the structural truth join;
- exact provenance hashes and finite metrics;
- zero language/model/API/training/registration/mutation/service/side-effect/action/execution access.

The gates are noncompensatory. No family, seed, selection rule, record, threshold, or split may be changed after the
design lock in response to V213 results.

## Decision

Passing authorizes only V214 deterministic candidate/version-space control design over the frozen V213 population.
It does not authorize a local/API model, protected downstream evaluation, external ontology payload, concept
registration, trusted-state mutation, a tool/service call, action, or execution.

Failure freezes a negative population-construction result and diagnoses the structural cause before any downstream
method is introduced.
