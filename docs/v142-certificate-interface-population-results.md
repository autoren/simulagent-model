# V142 Fresh Certificate Interface and Population Results

## Result

V142 freezes a fresh nine-choice controlled benchmark and a fail-closed evidence-certificate interface.
The population contains 48 six-stage groups and 288 fixtures, split evenly into 144 development and 144
test records. Every group includes familiar known wording, an unfamiliar paraphrase of the same known
capability, a clear novel-valid or unsupported alternative, an underdetermined request, and both targeted
clarification resolutions.

The catalog has four declared choices, three valid undeclared mechanics, one impossible retroactive-erasure
domain, and `A00`. Across the asset there are 48 familiar-known, 48 unfamiliar-known, 48 ambiguous, and 96
clarified fixtures. Novel-valid and unsupported alternatives both appear in clear and clarified form. Every
choice has hidden truth coverage.

Public and hidden fixture identifiers align exactly, no public record contains group, stage, language-class,
truth, or compatible-set labels, and every ambiguous record has exactly the registered two-choice compatible
set. Every decidable record has the exact singleton compatible set. No conversation is an exact duplicate of
V135 language.

The certificate validator enforces exact keys, typed sufficiency, a sorted unique nonempty compatible list,
and consistent proposal semantics. The deterministic finalizer always emits valid one-key choice JSON. A
valid singleton certificate produces that choice; ambiguity and every invalid, missing, truncated, or
inconsistent certificate produce `A00`. Certificates never prune the authoritative hypothesis universe and
never execute anything.

## Boundary and decision

V142 is project-authored synthetic development evidence, not externally authored transfer. Freeze the asset
and interface. The next permitted branch is model-free: prove that oracle certificates and deterministic
fallback reproduce the intended classification and sequential decision policy, then derive certificate-level
error gates. No language/model run is authorized yet. V134, external language, APIs, training, induction,
authority, action, and execution remain closed.
