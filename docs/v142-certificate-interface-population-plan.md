# V142 Fresh Certificate Interface and Population Plan

## Purpose

V142 prospectively defines the fresh controlled asset required after V141. It separates six language and
evidence stages within every group: familiar known wording, unfamiliar paraphrase of the same known
capability, a clear novel-valid or unsupported alternative, genuinely insufficient language, and both
clarification resolutions.

The nine-choice catalog contains four declared capabilities, three valid but undeclared mechanics, one
impossible retroactive-erasure domain, and `A00`. Every family contrasts a known candidate with a clean
alternative and supplies an exact targeted clarification question.

## Evidence certificate

The non-authoritative proposer must eventually emit exactly three typed fields: sufficiency status, a
sorted nonempty compatible-choice list, and a proposed choice. Sufficient certificates require one
compatible choice and a matching proposal. Insufficient certificates require at least two compatible
choices and `A00`.

The compatible list is evidence only and never prunes the complete authoritative hypothesis universe. A
deterministic finalizer—not the model—serializes the final one-choice JSON. Invalid, truncated, or
inconsistent certificates deterministically become `A00`, so syntax failure cannot produce an executable
choice or malformed final output.

## Population

Six families contain eight slot variants each, producing 48 six-stage groups and 288 fixtures. Each family
contributes four groups to development and four to test, for 144 fixtures per split. Public fixtures expose
only the complete catalog, fallible presented candidate, and conversation. Hidden records retain family,
stage, language class, compatible set, and exact truth.

## Boundary

This is project-authored synthetic development material. Passing authorizes only a model-free oracle and
policy audit of the certificate/finalizer semantics. It does not authorize a model run, V134 access,
external language, API use, training, induction, authority, action, or execution.
