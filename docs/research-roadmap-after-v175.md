# Research roadmap after V175

## Current evidence

V166–V170 established that exact posterior-aware evidence gathering can improve recommendation loss on the frozen
factored ontology problem. V171 confirmed the reversible stateful sandbox. V172–V173 then exposed an important
integration mismatch: a recommendation-optimal horizon-two planner never produced the exact consensus certificate
required by the trusted routing gate.

V174 measured that certificate geometry without policy scoring. V175 used the first feasible certificate horizon and
optimized the actual routed loss. The resulting mechanism was safe, beneficial, and pointwise dominant on the complete
V172 development population. This is strong development evidence, not fresh confirmation.

## Active research tracks

### Track A — open-world language and model proposals: dormant

The language branch remains scientifically negative or confounded at the required reliability level. No local model,
API model, prompt change, reasoning-budget change, ensemble, or novel-concept proposal should be introduced into the
active confirmation. The exact planner result does not repair semantic open-world recognition.

Reopen this track only with a separately preregistered utterance-level identifiability protocol and a target that is
observable from language. Model output may propose hypotheses or request clarification, but it may not register a
primitive, authorize a route, mutate trusted state, or execute.

### Track B — trusted reversible sandbox: confirmed support mechanism

The V171 contract remains unchanged: typed registered routes, optimistic revision checks, idempotent replay and
rollback, atomic multi-entity writes, provenance validation, restart verification, and exact oracle final state. This
track is reused as an integration boundary rather than retuned.

### Track C — certification-aware exact planning: positive development

Freeze the V175 policy exactly:

- five-query maximum horizon;
- query cost `1/10`;
- stop on equal routed risk;
- lowest valuation index on query ties;
- route only for unanimous alias or unanimous composition;
- defer mixed and unanimous provisional version spaces;
- no planner, model, oracle, or hidden-target commit authority.

No threshold, loss, cost, horizon, policy, or gate may be changed after seeing V175.

### Track D — fresh exact-signature-disjoint confirmation: next active track

Build a new project-authored population from four-constraint states. Before any policy score is computed:

1. enumerate the complete prespecified state frame;
2. retain states with at least two candidate classes and exact planner support;
3. construct every target case with the unchanged class-balanced weighting rule;
4. prove exact truth-signature disjointness from every V172 development target;
5. freeze the state and target membership plus population summary;
6. run the unchanged V175 policy and controls exactly once;
7. independently reconstruct every score and sandbox transition.

The primary confirmation claim is strict mean routed-risk improvement over immediate deferral and the unchanged V167
routed policy, with positive trusted completion and all noncompensatory safety gates. Dominance over greedy, random,
and open-loop controls is a strong secondary criterion. A safe negative or mixed result must be retained without
selection or tuning.

## After fresh confirmation

If the exact-signature-disjoint result confirms, the next useful question is robustness of the certification-aware
mechanism—not model capacity. Candidate branches include controlled observation noise, larger finite concept spaces,
or bounded misspecification, each with a new population and prospective gate. Only after those exact branches should
language proposals be reintroduced as an untrusted observation channel feeding the version space.

If confirmation fails, stop this mechanism branch and diagnose whether the cause is certificate depth, prior geometry,
or query cost using a new structural census. Do not repair the failed confirmation in place.
