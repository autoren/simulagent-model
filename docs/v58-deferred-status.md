# V58 deferred status

Status as of 2026-08-16: deferred without release or collection.

V58 remains a sealed, optional language-validation branch. Its narrow scientific question requires independently human-authored wording, real role separation, real consent and contribution provenance, and independent human semantic validation. No such participants are available to this project, so no coordinator declaration, pilot release lock, human submission, validation, adjudication, candidate parser, or V58 evaluation may be created.

The synthetic fixtures already exercised by the frozen V58 audits are software and protocol tests only. Model-generated utterances may be used in a separately named development stress test, but they may never be placed in V58 human-submission locations, accompanied by invented human attestations, or counted as evidence for human-authored surface transfer.

This deferral does not revise or weaken the frozen V48–V57 results. In particular, it does not block continued work on calibrated inference, experimental design, Bayes-adaptive planning, or verification. V58 may be resumed only if genuinely independent human writers, validators, an adjudicator, and a coordinator later become available under the existing release gate.
