# V177 certification-aware planner fresh-confirmation plan

## Frozen mechanism

V177 changes only the input population. It reuses V175's exact certification-aware dynamic program, horizon five,
query cost `1/10`, routed loss, stop and query tie breaks, seven policy set, deterministic unanimity gate, and V171
sandbox contract without alteration.

Four initial constraints leave at most four informative valuations. The horizon remains five because it is a fixed
maximum, not a mandate to issue five queries; policies stop earlier when their frozen rule says to stop.

## Fresh population

Evaluate every one of the 135 frozen V176 eligible states and every one of its 2,160 targets with class-balanced prior
weights. V176 was built and independently frozen with zero policy scores. Its exact target-context signatures do not
occur in V172/V175, although candidate identities come from the same ontology.

The target-informed oracle certificates are reconstructed exactly during confirmation solely for the already-frozen
non-operational comparator. They cannot select states, targets, policies, or commits.

## Decision

Primary confirmation requires safety plus mean routed risk strictly below immediate deferral and unchanged V167 routed
planning, positive trusted completion, and at least one state with strict improvement. Strong confirmation additionally
requires no worse mean risk than greedy, best fixed open-loop, and random-order controls, plus pointwise no-worse risk
than every operational control in every state.

All safety gates are noncompensatory. A safe negative or mixed result is frozen without population selection, horizon,
cost, loss, tie, gate, or policy changes. No model, API, registration, real service, real-state mutation, side effect,
or execution is allowed.
