# V119 Asymmetric Adaptive Evidence Plan

V119 is a language-free causal simulator derived from the locked V118 evidence requirements. It does not
repeat V117's general questions. A root candidate-confirmation observation routes to two identity-specific
mechanisms after `CONFIRM`, or to two support-status mechanisms after `REJECT`/`UNSURE`. Every path costs
0.30, matching V117.

All active mechanisms have the same swept marginal reliability. Their joint channel interpolates between
independent errors and a common all-mechanism correctness/error shock. The correlation-aware planner is
compared with a planner that assumes independence. All hypotheses remain in the posterior; novel and
insufficient hypotheses can cause abstention but can never become actions.

The frozen V117 all-prior gates are reused without relaxation. Even a pass would establish only simulator
feasibility. It would authorize a model-free audit of whether real, separately governed evidence sources
could instantiate the four mechanisms—not language/model generation, protected access, induction, or
execution.
