# V159 fresh controlled relational-grammar population results

## Result

V159 passed every preregistered population, freshness, access, and firewall gate.

The frozen asset contains 256 project-authored synthetic fixtures in 32 complete eight-stage groups. The
development and evaluation halves each contain 128 fixtures. Each of the eight stages and eight strata has
32 fixtures.

## Grammar separation

The four specific questions expose eight unique normalized relation aliases. Every `grammar_unique` request
contains one registered alias that belongs to one specific query. Every `grammar_conflict` request contains
two aliases belonging to two distinct specific queries and is labeled for generic routing. Insufficient
requests contain no relation alias.

These labels are hidden from public fixtures. Public requests contain only the conversation and an optional
closed-answer event. No truth state, compatible set, oracle query, parsed relation, relation cardinality,
witness, candidate-state proposal, confidence, or pruning field is public.

## Trust boundary

All 64 specific closed answers routed to their exact typed states. All 64 generic route answers were valid
and produced zero semantic witnesses. Requests, route-only answers, and all malformed events remained
`A00`. Every path retained the complete authoritative hypothesis universe and execution remained zero.

Exact conversation overlap with the seven prior controlled populations was zero. No policy was scored and
no model, API, training routine, service, side effect, action, or execution was used.

## Decision

Freeze the V159 population. Authorize only a separately preregistered model-free development policy that
uses the public relation-alias registry and visible request text. The policy must compare grammar-aware
routing with lexical retrieval, always-generic fallback, source order, no-query abstention, and a hidden
information oracle.

Do not open V159 evaluation, tune on development outcomes, introduce an LLM/API, fit calibration, induce a
capability, alter authoritative state, act, or execute.

## Claim boundary

This is a synthetic controlled-grammar design result. It does not show that natural language generally
follows the grammar, that aliases cover open-world paraphrases, or that the system is deployment-safe.
