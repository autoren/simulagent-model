# V70 confirmatory reporting and fallback diagnostics

The primary replication includes all 244 sealed records. A model qualifies for the paired MAP replication criterion only when at least one of its records simultaneously has an exact-Bayes-adaptive/MAP first-action disagreement and normalized MAP regret of at least 0.005. The overall and stratum thresholds are applied to this same qualified-model set, conservatively enforcing the confirmatory plan's paired wording.

Fallback diagnostics are frozen before outcome evaluation. They report usage, expected entry probability, material-regret overlap, and control-specific fallback-free subsets by model. These diagnostics cannot remove records, modify gates, reverse the primary decision, or select models.

The terminating and nonterminating cheese models remain individual novel Tier A environments. Their side-by-side Tier B stress comparison is descriptive and has no override gate or external-uncertainty-family claim.
