# V185 deterministic SGD candidate-set controls results

## Verdict

V185 is a clean negative deterministic-development result with complete safety. None of the 864 prospectively frozen joint threshold configurations qualified on calibration. The locked controller therefore used its always-generic fallback and closed the local-model successor.

The failure criterion was demanding but directly tied to the proposed use: a cheap candidate-specific clarification was allowed only if character n-gram schema retrieval and token/schema overlap returned the same singleton, with at least 95% specific-route precision, at most 5% false-specific routing on provisional or unsupported requests, and at least 20% known coverage. No configuration satisfied all three requirements.

## Evaluation result

Because calibration found no qualifying configuration, the frozen fallback made zero candidate-specific routes on the 66-record evaluation half:

- 60 observed requests received generic clarification;
- 6 missing controls remained insufficient without a query;
- mean clarification cost was `0.3636363636`, exactly the safe always-generic control;
- improvement over always generic was zero;
- known-specific coverage was zero;
- final exactness after trusted answers was 100%; and
- authoritative hypothesis retention was 100%.

The exact intent-alias comparator also made zero specific routes. The hidden information oracle demonstrates that useful selective routing exists: it routed all 36 known records specifically, made no false-specific route, and achieved mean cost `0.2818181818`. The negative result is therefore about the frozen observable deterministic signals, not about the absence of possible value.

## Residual and interpretation

The prediction-defined residual contains all 60 observed evaluation requests and covers known, provisional, and unsupported truths. Its membership uses predictions only. It is meaningful as a description of what the deterministic controls could not resolve, but it does not authorize a model run because the parent design required the deterministic router itself to qualify before handing off a residual.

This result reinforces the earlier V126/V127/V128 boundary in a stricter downstream role. Simple schema similarity and token overlap are not only unsafe as open-world classifiers; on this SGD population they are not sufficiently precise and selective even for choosing a cheaper candidate-confirmation question under a fail-closed controller.

## Safety and access

Every retrieval output remained a shadow set. A trusted typed clarification—not retrieval—determined every terminal state. Wrong or missing retrieval could not accept a known capability, declare novelty, reject a capability, prune the authoritative universe, register an ontology item, mutate trusted state, call a service, act, or execute.

The run read development language once and performed 720 character-view plus 720 token-view candidate scores. Protected language was not opened. Manual language inspection, model/API use, training, ontology registration, trusted-state mutation, service calls, side effects, action, and execution were zero.

Freeze:

`freeze_V185_boundary_result_without_protected_or_model_access`

Do not change the feature views, grids, calibration gates, costs, fallback, split, or residual; do not run a local/API model on this residual; and do not open protected language. A future language branch requires a genuinely different source of semantic evidence—such as independently grounded typed observations or trusted clarification answers—not another similarity threshold or prompt variation.
