# V123 Fresh-population Availability Audit Results

## Outcome

V123 passed as an exhaustion audit:

> `massive_unused_pool_insufficient_for_fresh_paired_transfer_require_external_source`

After excluding every V101, V112, V114, and V115 identifier, the MASSIVE test pool has 38 unused
known-familiar, 186 known-unfamiliar, 9 novel-valid, and 111 unsupported candidates. The maximum balanced
population is therefore only nine records per class. More importantly, all nine remaining novel-valid
candidates come from one scenario, while the frozen transfer requirement needs both novel-valid scenarios.

The planned 32-per-class paired retrieval-selectivity test is not feasible without reusing prior/protected
records, shrinking the benchmark to an uninformative sliver, or weakening scenario coverage after seeing
availability. None is permitted.

V123 read no language or individual candidate, evaluated no signal, loaded no model, and executed nothing.
The only authorized successor is a separately preregistered feasibility audit for a new controlled open-set
source. Schema-Guided Dialogue is the leading candidate because its service/intent schemas and held-out
services can support an explicit declared/undeclared/unsupported construction, subject to a source audit.
