# Research roadmap after V220

## Frozen conclusion

V220 is a verified-candidate positive fresh-population result on the untouched Mondo pair
`v2026-05-05 -> v2026-06-02`. Exact payload/control integrity and all direct population strata passed. The resulting
2,161-family population is large, event-diverse, ambiguity-rich, and decision-relevant. Development-only oracle checks
passed exactly; 540 protected groups remain sealed and unloaded for scoring.

This supports the next deterministic development track. It remains retrospective artifact evidence, not evidence of a
new speaker's intended meaning.

## V221: deterministic candidate and version-space residual study

V221 should be designed and audited before running a candidate method. It may open only V220 development public/truth
records and must leave every protected JSONL file sealed. The design should freeze:

1. a hash-derived, group-disjoint development calibration/evaluation role split;
2. deterministic inputs limited to public surface text, declared release evidence, and the published ontology-derived
   catalog available under the V220 claim boundary;
3. candidate generators in increasing complexity: normalized exact lookup, token/character retrieval, deterministic
   field parsing, lifecycle-link expansion, and bounded typed composition where expressible;
4. equivalence-class collapse and contradiction handling before any decision;
5. fixed candidate budgets, preferably `k in {1, 4, 8, 16}`;
6. oracle-class recall, complete version-space retention, candidate-set size, unsafe singleton collapse, exact decision,
   and decision regret by evidence mode and event stratum;
7. explicit `INSUFFICIENT` handling for unexpressible or conflicting evidence; and
8. a residual definition that requires at least 12 evaluation groups with decision-relevant oracle classes missing at a
   fixed accepted budget before model design is allowed.

V221 is diagnostic, not a competition to maximize top-1 accuracy. The principal output is the exact residual: which
representational distinctions deterministic controls cannot express or retrieve without unsafe collapse.

## Conditional V222 local-model track

Only a verified V221 residual may authorize a separately frozen local-model study. The model should receive the same
public evidence and a bounded deterministic context, produce typed candidates only, and run at a fixed candidate budget
and low reasoning effort. Invalid, truncated, or unsupported output becomes `INSUFFICIENT`.

Its success criterion is incremental oracle-class recall over the deterministic system without increasing unsafe
singleton collapse or downstream regret. A larger or combined model is not justified merely by top-1 improvement. An
API is optional and remains unnecessary unless a later local capacity study is independently warranted.

## Parallel B2c external-semantics track

Existing ontology releases can advance retrospective identification, version semantics, and decision consequences.
They cannot alone adjudicate whether a novel utterance is an unusual expression of a known capability or evidence of a
new one. Strong new-speaker B2c claims remain deferred pending appropriately sourced speaker/domain evidence. Synthetic
people may test the mechanics of an elicitation protocol but cannot validate that claim.

## Preserved boundaries

- V218 development/protected records remain excluded and sealed.
- V220 protected files remain hash-only until a separately justified confirmation protocol.
- V216 and V213 protected data remain sealed.
- No model, training, registration, trusted mutation, service action, or execution is authorized.
- No deterministic threshold or residual gate may be tuned after V221 evaluation exposure.
