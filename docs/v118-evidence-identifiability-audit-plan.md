# V118 Evidence Identifiability Audit Plan

V118 is a locked, aggregate-only algebraic audit of the V117 negative result. It introduces no new
observation channel and does not sweep prompts or tune reliability. It derives the posterior action regions
implied by the frozen cost matrix, converts them to prior-dependent Bayes-factor requirements, and measures
the effective likelihood ratios already present in the frozen V117 channel.

The audit must explain three boundaries before another simulator is justified: why the uniform prior cannot
support an exact known action, why the strong candidate prior can suppress an unsupported action under
correlated failures, and whether finite asymmetric evidence could cross both boundaries. It also derives the
perfect-observation ceiling imposed by candidate coverage.

A successful audit authorizes only preregistration of a new language-free adaptive causal simulator. That
future design must allocate different evidence to candidate identity and support status. V118 cannot
authorize language/model generation, prompt selection, protected access, induction, action authority, or
execution.
