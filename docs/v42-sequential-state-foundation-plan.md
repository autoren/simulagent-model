# V42 oracle sequential-state foundation

## Why this is next

V41 confirms the current architecture within its declared one-step Boolean relational scope. The next scientific question should therefore require a capability that architecture does not contain: persistent state that changes across an ordered sequence of actions.

V42 isolates this break before adding stochastic effects, delayed consequences, active experiment choice, open concepts, or language uncertainty. Combining those axes immediately would make failures uninterpretable.

## Question and claim boundary

V42 asks whether a minimal stateful symbolic extension can represent, identify, and execute deterministic multi-action mechanics over variable entities and directed relations, including cases where action order changes the final observation.

It is an oracle-first development foundation. All states, actions, and ontology symbols are supplied directly. A pass does not establish language grounding or general world-model learning.

## Population

The population contains 40 mechanics, ten from each of:

- set, clear, and toggle effects;
- effects conditioned on the current state;
- directed relational propagation; and
- order-sensitive compositions.

Sequences have two, three, or four actions over two to five entities. Support identifies each mechanic from no more than 16 sequences; each mechanic has 24 structurally fresh query sequences. Fit contains 24 mechanics and open development evaluation contains 16 mechanics. Mechanics—not individual transitions—are the reporting unit.

Every order-sensitive mechanic has paired queries with the same initial state and action multiset in different orders. At least one registered pair per mechanic must have a different final observation, making persistence and order causally necessary.

## Minimal stateful DSL

Conditions reuse the typed Boolean expression foundation: unary and relational atoms, negation, conjunction, disjunction, and one existential variable. Effects add set-true, set-false, toggle, copy, and conditional effects. Effects within one action are simultaneous; the resulting state persists into the next action.

Partial states use explicit unknown values. The executor returns every possible next or final observation across compatible complete worlds and surviving mechanics.

## Controls

The primary system is exact version-space induction over the stateful DSL. The frozen V22 one-step executor is a registered inadequacy control: it may inspect the initial state and action sequence but cannot carry mutations between actions. Literal sequence lookup is the non-lifted control and must fail on structurally fresh queries.

The primary system must be exact overall, for every family and sequence length, and on order counterfactuals. The memoryless control must score at most 0.850 and lookup at most 0.950. If either control passes, the benchmark has not made stateful generalization necessary.

## Decision

- Stateful passes and memoryless fails: authorize a separate sequential-language grounding experiment.
- Stateful fails: repair the DSL, generator, or executor before language or neural work.
- Memoryless passes: redesign the benchmark’s causal necessity.
- Lookup passes: redesign the generalization split.

Stochasticity and delay are the next isolated extensions only after this foundation passes.
