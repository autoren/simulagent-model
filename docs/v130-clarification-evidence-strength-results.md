# V130 Clarification Evidence-strength Audit Results

## Outcome

V130 completed every preregistered calculation and passed the abstract single-source feasibility rule:

> `complete_clarification_evidence_strength_feasible_authorize_realization_audit_only`

All nine one-answer conditions had a finite threshold on the locked 0.0005 grid. Six passed at 95%. Uniform
candidate-attracted error required 96%; strong candidate-attracted error required 96.7%; and the strongest
boundary was 97.25% for a strong wrong-candidate prior with symmetric error. This is below the prospectively
locked 99% feasibility ceiling.

Multiple 95%-correct answers were much more fragile. With zero common-shock dependence, two answers passed
the conditions that one answer failed. At correlation 0.25 or 0.50, however, none of one, two, or three
answers passed the hardest strong-prior symmetric and candidate-attracted conditions; uniform-prior
candidate attraction also failed. Consequently the multi-source route failed its required-correlation gate.

The distinction is important:

- one genuinely 97.25%-reliable complete answer is sufficient in this abstract channel;
- two 95%-reliable answers are sufficient only when their errors are genuinely independent;
- adding more answers cannot wash out even moderate shared failure under the frozen common-shock model.

This is model-free evidence geometry, not evidence that a real source reaches 97.25% reliability. Passing
authorizes only a separately preregistered realization audit that measures an actual typed-answer source
against this fixed boundary. Repeated samples, paraphrases, or small/large variants from one model family
cannot be presumed independent. Language, human, or model evaluation remains closed until that design is
frozen; protected access, capability induction, richer planning, APIs, training, authority, and execution
remain closed.
