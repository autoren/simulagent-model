# V53r1 preregistration: pre-population PMCMC and joint-resolution repair

V53’s design was frozen before implementation, population construction, or evaluation. Altered-seed implementation fixtures then exposed two design-level mismatches.

First, the frozen PMCMC logit proposal standard deviation of `0.35` produced acceptance rates from `0.807` to `0.900` across four families, inconsistent with the unchanged non-compensatory acceptance gate `[0.10, 0.70]`. Fixed altered-seed trials at standard deviations `0.7`, `0.9`, `1.1`, and `1.3` gave mean acceptance `0.733`, `0.657`, `0.607`, and `0.554`, respectively. V53r1 fixes the proposal standard deviation at `1.30`; its observed altered-seed range was `0.493–0.627`. No proposal adaptation is permitted.

Second, a single 509-particle posterior represented with 20 equal-width theta bins had mean joint `(M, theta)` TV `0.09088`. Even the equal-weight mixture of three independent repeats measured `0.07147` on four altered-seed records, above the frozen `0.06` mean gate. This is the expected finite-histogram resolution problem: 20 theta cells per program ask a 509-particle approximation to populate substantially more cells than the downstream scientific comparison needs. Ten equal-width bins gave mean TV `0.05734` in a single-repeat eight-record fixture. V53r1 therefore freezes 10 equal-width theta bins over `[0.05, 0.95]` and defines each budget-level estimate as the equal-weight posterior mixture across the already-preregistered three independent repeats. Particle counts remain 31, 127, and 509 per repeat.

The repair also records implementation details that were previously implicit. Continuous probabilities are executed only through V53-local transition and filtering functions, leaving the frozen finite-probability V46–V52 validators untouched. The exhausted cross-action candidate pool is extended with the symmetric `set_false` stochastic effect while retaining its action topology. Exact and approximate inference expose joint `(program, theta, hidden configuration)` atoms so SBC never constructs a false product of marginals.

All population quotas, random roots, exact oracle, SMC² structure, PMCMC chain lengths, metrics, gates, controls, firewalls, and claim boundaries remain unchanged. Candidate populations still may not be constructed until the V53r1 implementation passes an altered-seed audit and a new implementation lock is frozen. Active expected-information-gain work remains blocked.
