# Research roadmap after V185

## Boundary now established

The project has two independently confirmed pieces and one newly closed handoff:

1. V175/V177 and V180/V182 establish an exact, certification-aware fixed-ontology planner that remains safe and cost-effective under a declared one-corruption binary channel when each query is tripled and majority decoded.
2. V171 establishes the deterministic reversible sandbox and keeps all commit authority outside learned components.
3. V183–V185 test the first externally grounded semantic-observation handoff after that confirmation.

V183 replaced SGD intent-name/domain-composite labels with 14 semantic capability contracts: 6 declared known, 6 valid-undeclared provisional, and 2 unsupported. Service version labels were excluded, exact duplicates were deduplicated, no contract crossed truth kinds, and the 240 source records split cleanly into 120 development and 120 protected identities plus 12 missing controls per role.

V184 extracted the two language roles and six visible known schemas exactly into immutable artifacts. Gold service, intent, contract, compatibility, and evaluation fields are absent from language records. Protected language remains unread during development.

V185 then tested whether two deterministic schema-similarity views could safely choose a cheaper candidate-confirmation question. None of 864 frozen calibration configurations simultaneously achieved 95% specific-route precision, at most 5% non-known false-specific routing, and at least 20% known coverage. The locked fallback asked generic clarification for all observed evaluation requests. Safety and final exactness were complete, but there was no selectivity or cost benefit. The local-model residual route is therefore closed, and protected language remains sealed.

The supported conclusion is:

> Source labels and capability contracts can be made coherent, but ungrounded schema similarity does not supply sufficiently reliable semantic evidence—even for question routing. The next useful object is a typed clarification channel whose answers have explicit semantics, not another classifier, threshold, prompt, or model scale.

## Active next track: finite typed contract clarification

Construct a model-free finite question codebook over the 14 frozen SGD capability contracts. This is an interface and planning study, not a claim that existing utterances or an LLM answer the questions reliably.

### Required sequence

1. **Question-codebook feasibility.** Derive candidate questions only from frozen contract attributes: normalized intent concepts, slot presence, transactionality, and explicitly allowed domain/support properties. Exclude service version labels and presented candidates. Prove whether every pair of contracts is separable; retain observationally equivalent contracts as one set.
2. **Exact clean planning.** On every V183 development version space, compare exact adaptive question selection with open-loop, greedy information gain, source order, always-generic clarification, and a target-informed oracle. Charge explicit question and deferral costs.
3. **Answer provenance boundary.** Source annotations may instantiate an oracle answer for simulation, but must be reported as dataset-provided structured labels—not human answers, deployed sensors, or model evidence. Paraphrases or repeated calls to one model are not independent observations.
4. **Correlated-error stress.** Before any learned answer proposal, evaluate common-shock and adversarially correlated errors. A single-pass answer may never support trusted routing if the declared error model permits a corrupt decisive answer.
5. **Robust coding and confirmation.** If the clean codebook has value and the one-corruption feasibility audit passes, reuse the proven triple-repetition/majority wrapper as a frozen mechanism and confirm on the protected identity role without reading protected utterance language. If the codebook is not identifying or not cost-effective, freeze the negative result.
6. **Later learned observation, only if independently justified.** A local model may eventually propose answer sets or question rankings only after a distinct development population and protocol establish why it adds evidence beyond V185's failed similarity views. Malformed or truncated output is `INSUFFICIENT`. An API remains optional and non-authoritative.

## Track status

- Fixed finite ontology, exact planner, sandbox, and bounded one-corruption robustness: confirmed and closed for in-place tuning.
- Externally grounded contract/population identity: passed at V183.
- Role-isolated SGD language extraction: passed at V184; protected language sealed.
- Deterministic similarity-based question routing: failed at V185 and closed.
- Local/API residual proposer on V185: not authorized.
- Typed contract clarification and robust planning: active.
- Capability registration, trusted-state mutation from language, real service calls, side effects, action, and execution: forbidden.

## Durable safety rule

Language, retrieval, and models may influence which question is asked or may increase deferral. They may not accept a capability, remove authoritative hypotheses, register a provisional concept, commit a state mutation, invoke a service, or execute. Only deterministic validation of a trusted typed observation may update the exact version space; the reversible sandbox remains the sole commit boundary.
